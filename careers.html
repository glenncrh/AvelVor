Careers at AvelVor

Since 1958, our family boatyard has built boats in the port of Roscoff. Each year, we welcome engineering students for internships: real projects, a team that shares its know-how, and boats you can test at sea.

  • Design office

    Naval Architecture Engineering Intern

    Roscoff · 6-month internship · From March 2027 · Ref. AV-NA-27
    See the offer

    Join our design office and take part in the design of the next evolution of our range, from the first lines plan to the prototype in the yard.

    Your missions

    • Draw hull lines plans and general arrangements for a new version of the Abadenn
    • Carry out weight, centre of gravity and stability calculations, and check them against the ISO standards for small craft
    • Prepare the drawings and documents used by our moulding and wood finishing workshops
    • Follow the build of the first prototype in the yard and take part in its sea trials
    • Suggest design improvements based on feedback from our customers and workshops

    Your profile

    • Final-year engineering student specialising in naval architecture or mechanical engineering
    • Good command of a 3D CAD tool (Rhino, SolidWorks or similar); Maxsurf or a similar hull design tool is a plus
    • Rigorous, curious and at ease working with workshop teams
    • Interest in boats and the sea; working level of English

    Send your CV and cover letter to avelvor1@gmail.com with the reference AV-NA-27 in the subject line.

  • Research & development

    Naval Hydrodynamics Engineering Intern

    Roscoff · 6-month internship · From March 2027 · Ref. AV-HY-27
    See the offer

    Help us understand and improve how our hulls behave in the water, especially the Morvran, our largest boat built for the open sea.

    Your missions

    • Estimate the resistance and powering of our hulls to help choose the right engines
    • Run CFD simulations of hull variants and compare their performance
    • Analyse sea trial data (speed, trim, fuel consumption) and compare it with the simulations
    • Study the seakeeping behaviour of the Morvran in offshore conditions
    • Present recommendations to improve hull shapes and reduce fuel consumption

    Your profile

    • Final-year engineering student in fluid mechanics, hydrodynamics or naval engineering
    • Experience with a CFD tool (OpenFOAM, STAR-CCM+ or similar)
    • Data processing with Python or MATLAB
    • Analytical mind and taste for testing at sea; working level of English

    Send your CV and cover letter to avelvor1@gmail.com with the reference AV-HY-27 in the subject line. Or call +33 7 81 33 88 39

Why intern with us?

Real responsibilities

You work on projects that end up on the water: a new hull, a sea trial, a boat show stand.

A team that passes on its skills

In a company of 24 people, you work side by side with engineers, carpenters and moulding technicians, with a tutor who follows you.

Close to the sea

Our yard is in the port of Roscoff. Every intern takes part in at least one sea trial.
